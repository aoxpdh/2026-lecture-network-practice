Floor argument:
Assume an initially empty cache, the supplied per-name upstream interface,
fixed fixture TTLs, and no use of an expired answer or advance knowledge of values.
For each name, the first uncovered request forces a fetch. That fetch covers
requests only until its expiration. At the first request at or after expiration,
another fetch is mandatory. Fetching earlier only moves expiration earlier and
cannot cover a later request that this greedy schedule could not cover.
Summing these per-name mandatory TTL intervals gives 275. Retaining fresh
entries without eviction attains the bound. Fewer requests would require stale
reuse, preloaded knowledge, or a different upstream interface.
